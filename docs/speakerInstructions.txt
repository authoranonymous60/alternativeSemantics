Recording Instructions

The recording should take around 30 minutes. I’ve attached the three
files you should record. There’s also an audio file where I recorded a
series of sentences, just to give you an idea.

What you are recording

You will be recording a series of 24 sentence pairs. Each pair
consists of two short sentences. There are 3 files, with 8 sentence
pairs in each file. Here is an example of a sentence pair:

Sam only gave ELLEN oranges. 
Sam also gave Sue oranges.

The most important thing

In each S1, one word is written in UPPERCASE. You must say that word
with stronger emphasis than the other words — stress it slightly more
than you would in normal speech. The emphasis should sound natural,
not exaggerated. Practice a few times before recording.  Listen to the
audio file that I’ve attached to get an idea of how to stress the
uppercase word.

Before you start

Find a quiet room with no background noise — turn off fans, TV, etc.
Use your phone's built-in voice memo app.  Hold the phone a
comfortable distance from your mouth — about 8-12 inches Do a short
test recording of one or two sentences and listen back to check the
audio sounds clear before doing the full set. Send me the test clip
before proceeding, so I can confirm the quality is good.

Recording format

The 24 sentence pairs are divided into 3 sets of 8. Please record each
set as a separate audio file. Read at a natural, relaxed pace. If you
make a mistake or stumble on a sentence, just pause briefly and repeat
that sentence before continuing — do not stop the recording.

Sending the files

When you are done, send me the three audio files you recorded. WAV,
M4A or MP3 is fine.
